Directions

Try to score as many points as possible in seconds. If you last all 30 seconds, you can save the game to your personal bests (kept in this browser only). Click on the canvas to start the game, and also to pause it. You have 3 lives. You lose 200 points for your first death and 400 for your second. Your score is number of points multiplied by your shooting percentage.

Keys:

  • fire f key or space bar
  • left left arrow or h
  • right right arrow or l
  • down down arrow or j
  • up up arrow or k
  • touch screen use the arrow pad and FIRE button under the game

Score = points * percentage

Percentage = hits / shots
