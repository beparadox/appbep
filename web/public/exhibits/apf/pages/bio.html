About Me

     It was a dark, ominous New Year's Eve day way back in the year 1978, when Bart Starr was coaching the Packers and Brett Favre was just a little hellion raising a ruckus in Mississsssissssssippi. A little over a week earlier, on the 23rd of December, my parent's bravely ventured out of Northeast WI for a Christmas holiday family gathering in northeast Indiana at my Aunt Peg's place in Michigan City. I say 'bravely ventured' as my mother was 9 months pregnant with me, and against doctors orders decided to visit her sister. Many of you may find this irresponsible of her, but there are hospitals all along their planned travel route, and plus my mother, in addition to being one of the finest human beings the Good Lord ever thought to breathe life into, is tougher than lips on a woodpecker.

After a week of stuffing themselves silly and downing copious amounts of alcohol, particularly brandy and whiskey, it was time to head back to the God's City aka The Promise Land aka Green Bay. Along the way they were sidetracked by a horrible blizzard that hit the Chicago area--obviously a devious plot fiendishly devised by Satan himself to ensure I'd have something to be ashamed about for the rest of my life--and my mother went into labor right then and there, forcing my father to stop at the nearest hospital.

     Pa, who was hellbent on reaching Green Bay to attend Old Man Milner's annual New Year's Even bash, obviously wasn't too pleased, but what could he do with his child about to be born? He told my Mom "Can't you hold out till we cross the border into Wisconsin? Just give me 20 minutes!" cuz he was damned if his first born was going to come into the world in IL. But the snow was so heavy the traffic came to a standstill, and they had to get to the nearest hospital (They won't tell me which one, and frankly I don't care to find out.)

Pa was elated to have a son as his first-born, but he was mighty peeved about missing that party. He still brings up how he missed that party to this day. He blames me for missing that party, yeah genius like it's my fault you got Mom pregnant and decided to drive 300 plus miles when she was due. Besides Milner has thrown a wicked New Years Eve party every year for the last several years, like the one in 1995 when I had just turned 16 and got really drunk for the first time, stole the old-man's keys took Susie Schmidt on a little spin down to the creek where the car got stuck so we hopped in the back with the engine running and she started kissing me on the d.... uh, let's just say I got in deep trouble. But yeah these partys are always swinging events, fo' sho'.

"But 78' was special" he's told me again and again. "That's the year John Ferguson got shitfaced and ended up bare-assed naked singing Christmas Carols in the middle of the street. Almost died of pnuemonia." Is that supposed to make me feel guilty? Hell, that ain't nothin', Ferguson always gets wasted and gets buck-nekked, that's just what he does. Truth is, Pops was never pleased I was born in Chicago. He has some deep seated hatred for that city after an incident back in the 60's with some rowdy Bears fans, but I digress.
